The Fintech Board Checklist

What a director watches in eight fintech sub-sectors: paytech, lending, embedded finance, digital assets, wealthtech, regtech, insurtech and capital markets.

Built from Module 3 of the FINTECH Circle Board Programme.

How to use it

Start with where the company sits

Every fintech has a position in the regulated value chain: front-end apps and distribution; product manufacturer (credit, loans, insurance, investment products); infrastructure enablement (processing, APIs, compliance tooling); or the data and identity layer.

Then read the checklist for its sub-sector before the first board meeting, and bring the questions it raises.

The metrics differ too. Consumer fintech: CAC and payback period, lifetime value and retention, active use and churn timing, revenue diversification. B2B fintech: ARR, net revenue retention, contract length, renewal rates, top-client concentration.
